Satellite convergence

Passes over each hotspot in the next 12 hours vs the usual for this time. Countries move satellites to revisit places they care about; a jump of 1.4× or more counts as a warning sign.

GPS degradation by region

Military activity

Military aircraft visible on public flight tracking, last 14 days. Sudden drops can mean transponders switched off before operations; surges in transports can mean evacuations or deployments.

Sector signals

Stock baskets vs their market over 5 trading days. Before COVID, mask, hazmat and vaccine makers jumped weeks before markets fell; defence stocks tend to rise as conflicts escalate. Outperformance by several points is a corroborating sign, never proof on its own.

Learned patterns

Each time a real event happens (a strike, a blockade, a nationwide outage), the system records which warning signs came first and how many hours ahead. Over months this shows which signs actually predict what.
