Cyber attack map

Where attacks are landing and where they come from, by country. Hit: ransomware victims posted on leak sites (last 7 days). From: the top 100 attacking addresses seen by SANS sensors worldwide, and botnet command servers. Attack origins only show where the traffic comes from — attackers often route through other countries, so this is not proof of who is behind it. The animated lines show the overall flow from the main origin countries to the most-hit countries; our data does not link individual attacks, so a line does not mean that country attacked that one.

Undersea cables: loitering vessels

Ships moving under 3 knots for 45+ minutes inside known cable corridors (Australia, Taiwan, the Baltic, the Red Sea), from live ship-tracking (AIS). Cables near Taiwan and in the Baltic have been cut by ships dragging anchors, including Chinese- and Hong Kong-linked vessels. Ships can switch tracking off and coverage far offshore is patchy, so an empty list is not proof of safety.

Learned patterns
