          Make emergency alerts loud

          Emergency alerts (CFS Emergency Warnings, Watch and Act near home, Level 5) are sent at the highest priority and repeated twice over nine minutes. For them to sound at night, each phone needs one setting changed:

          • Android: Settings → Apps → ntfy → Notifications → "Max priority" → turn on Override Do Not Disturb, and pick a loud sound.
          • iPhone: Settings → Focus → Do Not Disturb (and Sleep) → Apps → add ntfy. Keep the ring switch on at night; ntfy cannot sound through the mute switch.
          • A loud drill is sent at 10 am on the first Saturday of January, April, July and October to prove it works.
